ABOUT NEON NEXUS

More Than A Place. A Space To Belong.

NEON NEXUS is a modern entertainment and creative hub designed for teenagers and young adults who are looking for a place to connect, create, learn, and have fun.

01 / OUR STORY

Where It All Began

Founded in 2026 in Gqeberha, South Africa, NEON NEXUS was created to provide a safe and welcoming environment where young people can socialise, explore their creativity, and participate in a wide variety of activities.

We bring together people who share interests in gaming, music, art, food, and community engagement, creating a space where everyone feels welcome and inspired.

02 / MISSION

Our Mission

Our goal is to become a place that all young people can recognise as a safe space to be themselves to their fullest extent and experience genuine community.

03 / VISION

Our Vision

We aim to become a leading entertainment and creative hub across South Africa, known for uniting communities through recreational activities and a welcoming environment for people of all backgrounds.
