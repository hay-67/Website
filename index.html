Welcome to NEON NEXUS

Welcome to Gqberha's ultimate entertainment and creative hub. NEON NEXUS brings together gaming, music, art, food, and community all in one exciting space for teens and young adults.

Who We Are

NEON NEXUS was founded in 2026 and created to provide teenagers and young adults with a safe and welcoming environment where they can socialise, express themselves, and connect with others who share similar interests.

What We Offer

  • Console Gaming Area
  • Music Practice and Recording Studio
  • Community Art and Graffiti Wall
  • Study and Relaxation Lounge
  • Darts and Billiards
  • Board Games
  • Live Events and Tournaments
  • NEXUS Cafe
