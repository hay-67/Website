WELCOME TO THE NEXUS

Where Ideas
Come Alive.

Welcome to Gqberha's ultimate entertainment and creative hub. NEON NEXUS brings together gaming, music, art, food, and community all in one exciting space for teens and young adults.

PLAY • CREATE • CONNECT

01 / ABOUT

Who We Are

NEON NEXUS was founded in 2026 and created to provide teenagers and young adults with a safe and welcoming environment where they can socialise, express themselves, and connect with others who share similar interests.

02 / EXPERIENCES

What We Offer

Explore the different spaces and activities available throughout the NEON NEXUS experience.

Console Gaming

Play your favourite games and compete with friends in our dedicated gaming area.

Music Studio

Practice, create and record music in our fully equipped studio spaces.

Creative Art

Express yourself through drawing, painting, graffiti and collaborative artwork.

Study Lounge

Take a break, study or relax in a comfortable shared environment.

Darts & Billiards

Challenge your friends to a casual game of darts or billiards.

Board Games

Enjoy classic and modern board games with friends and other members.

Live Events

NEXUS Cafe
